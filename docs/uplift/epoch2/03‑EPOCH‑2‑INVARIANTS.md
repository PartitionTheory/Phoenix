Phoenix Epoch 2 — Invariants (Uplift Edition)
Epoch 2 introduces the first invariant structures within Phoenix. Invariants describe mathematical behaviours that remain stable across transformations, operator mappings, and domain transitions. These constructs allow Phoenix to maintain coherence even as it expands into multi‑domain algebra and synthetic time.

Phoenix invariants define stable relationships between operators, structural elements, and identity constructs. They ensure that transformations applied in later epochs do not break the underlying mathematical consistency established in Epoch 1.

Invariants are essential for Phoenix’s evolution. They form the backbone of the synthetic mathematics system, enabling recursion, structural generalisation, and distributed lifting. By establishing invariants early, Phoenix ensures that all future epochs inherit a stable mathematical foundation.
