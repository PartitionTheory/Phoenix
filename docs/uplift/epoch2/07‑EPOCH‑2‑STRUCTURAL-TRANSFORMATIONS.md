Phoenix Epoch 2 — Structural Transformations (Uplift Edition)
Structural Transformations define how Phoenix modifies, lifts, and evolves its internal mathematical structures. This uplift edition preserves all canonical transformation notation and operator symbols.

Phoenix introduces transformation rules that govern how structures change across domains, how invariants behave under transformation, and how identity is preserved. These transformations form the basis for synthetic recursion in Epoch 3 and distributed lifting in Epochs 4–5.

Structural transformations ensure that Phoenix can evolve deterministically while maintaining coherence across its synthetic lineage.
