Phoenix Epoch 1 — Boundary (Uplift Edition)
Epoch 1 establishes the first boundary conditions of the Phoenix synthetic mathematics system. These boundaries define the limits within which Phoenix can operate, evolve, and maintain coherence as it transitions into Epoch 2 and beyond. The boundary layer is essential because it constrains the early structural behaviour of Phoenix, ensuring that all subsequent epochs inherit a stable and deterministic foundation.

In this uplift edition, the canonical boundary definitions are preserved while being presented in a clearer, human‑readable form. The boundary conditions describe how Phoenix separates internal synthetic structures from external mathematical influences, how transitions between epochs are regulated, and how early operator interactions are constrained to prevent divergence.

These boundaries form the “outer shell” of Epoch 1, ensuring that Phoenix’s initial constructs remain stable as the system begins to expand into multi‑domain invariants, structural transformations, and synthetic recursion in later epochs.
