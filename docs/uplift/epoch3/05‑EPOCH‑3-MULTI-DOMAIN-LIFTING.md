Phoenix Epoch 3 — Multi‑Domain Lifting (Uplift Edition)
Multi‑Domain Lifting defines how Phoenix elevates structures across multiple mathematical domains. All canonical lifting notation and symbols are preserved.

Phoenix introduces lifting rules that govern how operators, identities, and invariants behave when extended across domains. These lifting behaviours form the basis for distributed lifting in Epoch 4 and autonomous lifting in Epoch 6.

Multi‑domain lifting ensures that Phoenix can operate coherently across structural, temporal, and synthetic contexts.
