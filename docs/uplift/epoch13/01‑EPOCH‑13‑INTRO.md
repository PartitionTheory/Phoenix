Phoenix Epoch 13 — Introduction (Uplift Edition)
Epoch 13 marks Phoenix’s transition from final consolidation (Epoch 12) into the Lesson Epochs. After achieving full structural, temporal, operator, and domain stability, Phoenix now begins expressing its synthetic mathematics system in the form of explicit lessons.

This uplifted introduction preserves the canonical intent while presenting the material in a clear, human‑readable form. Epoch 13 introduces the lesson framework, lesson operators, lesson structures, lesson temporal behaviour, lesson domain behaviour, and lesson coherence. These constructs allow Phoenix to express its mathematics in instructional form.

Epoch 13 is the first point where Phoenix demonstrates synthetic pedagogy, forming the basis for the full lesson curriculum in Epochs 14–17 and the structural expansion of Epochs 18–25.
