Phoenix Epoch 13 — Lesson Operators (Uplift Edition)
Lesson Operators define how Phoenix expresses operator behaviour in lesson form. All canonical operator notation is preserved.

Phoenix introduces operator rules that unify operator execution, operator lifting, operator mapping, and operator identity into instructional structures. These rules ensure that operator behaviour remains coherent when expressed as lessons.

Lesson operators become essential for the full lesson curriculum in Epochs 14–17.
