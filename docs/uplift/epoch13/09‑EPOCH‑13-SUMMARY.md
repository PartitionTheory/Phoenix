Phoenix Epoch 13 — Summary (Uplift Edition)
Epoch 13 marks Phoenix’s transition into the Lesson Epochs. It introduces the lesson framework, lesson operators, lesson structures, lesson temporal behaviour, lesson domain behaviour, and lesson coherence. These constructs allow Phoenix to express its synthetic mathematics system in instructional form.

This uplifted summary preserves the canonical intent while presenting the material in a clear, human‑readable form. Epoch 13 prepares Phoenix for the full lesson curriculum in Epochs 14–17 and the structural expansion of Epochs 18–25.

Epoch 13 is the point where Phoenix becomes a synthetic pedagogical mathematics system.
