Phoenix Epoch 5 — Autonomous Distributed Lifting (Uplift Edition)
Autonomous Distributed Lifting defines how Phoenix elevates structures across distributed environments without central coordination. All canonical lifting notation and symbols are preserved.

Phoenix introduces autonomous lifting rules that govern how operators, identities, and invariants behave when extended across machines. These lifting behaviours form the basis for autonomous behavioural execution in Epoch 6 and totality lifting in Epoch 8.

Autonomous distributed lifting ensures that Phoenix can operate coherently across structural, temporal, and synthetic contexts even when acting independently.
