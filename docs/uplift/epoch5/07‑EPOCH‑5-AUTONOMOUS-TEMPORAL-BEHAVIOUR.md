Phoenix Epoch 5 — Autonomous Temporal Behaviour (Uplift Edition)
Autonomous Temporal Behaviour extends the synthetic time construct introduced in Epoch 2 and distributed in Epoch 4 into autonomous environments. All canonical temporal notation is preserved.

Phoenix defines how synthetic time behaves independently across machines, how temporal coherence is maintained, and how autonomous time streams unify under synthetic consensus.

Autonomous temporal behaviour becomes essential for synthetic behavioural execution in Epoch 6.
