Phoenix Epoch 4 — Distributed Synthetic Time (Uplift Edition)
Distributed Synthetic Time extends the synthetic time construct introduced in Epoch 2 into distributed environments. All canonical temporal notation is preserved.

Phoenix defines how synthetic time behaves across machines, how temporal coherence is maintained, and how distributed time streams unify under synthetic consensus. Distributed synthetic time becomes essential for autonomous behaviour in Epoch 5 and totality in Epoch 8.
