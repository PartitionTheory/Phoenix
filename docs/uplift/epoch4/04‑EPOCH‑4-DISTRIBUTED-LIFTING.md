Phoenix Epoch 4 — Distributed Lifting (Uplift Edition)
Distributed Lifting defines how Phoenix elevates structures across multiple machines and distributed environments. All canonical lifting notation and symbols are preserved.

Phoenix introduces lifting rules that govern how operators, identities, and invariants behave when extended across distributed systems. These lifting behaviours form the basis for autonomous distributed lifting in Epoch 5 and totality lifting in Epoch 8.

Distributed lifting ensures that Phoenix can operate coherently across structural, temporal, and synthetic contexts in distributed environments.
