Phoenix Epoch 4 — Synthetic Consensus (Uplift Edition)
Synthetic Consensus defines how Phoenix achieves agreement across distributed machines. All canonical consensus notation is preserved.

Synthetic consensus becomes essential for autonomous distributed behaviour in Epoch 5 and totality in Epoch 8.
